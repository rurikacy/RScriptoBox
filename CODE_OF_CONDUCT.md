# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 中文

本准则适用于本项目的 Issues、Pull Requests、代码审查及其他交流。尊重参与者的背景和经验，围绕事实、具体问题与可复现信息讨论，欢迎提问和不同意见。

禁止骚扰、歧视、人身攻击、威胁、恶意刷屏，以及未经同意公开他人的身份、联系方式、私人脚本或日志。

可通过平台举报功能报告公开违规内容，或请求维护者处理。涉及隐私时不要重复公开敏感材料；若没有可用的私下联系方式，可创建不含细节的 Issue 请求联系渠道。安全问题遵循 [安全说明](SECURITY.md)。

维护者可根据情况提醒、移除内容、锁定讨论或限制参与，并尽量保护报告者隐私。有异议时，可通过同一渠道提供事实和理由请求复核。

## English

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project discussions. Respect different backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss concrete problems and reproducible evidence; questions and disagreement are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, spam and unauthorized disclosure of personal information, private scripts or logs are prohibited.

Use platform reporting tools or ask the maintainer to address public violations. Do not repeat sensitive material publicly. If no private channel is available, open an issue without details to request one. Follow [Security](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

Maintainers may issue reminders, remove content, lock discussions or restrict participation while protecting reporter privacy where possible. Request review through the same channel with relevant facts and reasons.
